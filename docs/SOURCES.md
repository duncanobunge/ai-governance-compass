## Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 (No. 24 of 2019)

- Official consolidated text (Kenya Law / National Council for Law Reporting): https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/2019/24
- Data Protection (General) Regulations, 2021: https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/THE-DATA-PROTECTION-GENERAL-REGULATIONS-2021-1.pdf
- Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC): https://www.odpc.go.ke/
- Sections cited in this dataset:
  - Sec. 25 — Principles of Personal Data Protection
  - Sec. 26 — Rights of a Data Subject
  - Sec. 29 — Duty to Notify
  - Sec. 30 — Lawful Processing of Personal Data
  - Sec. 31 — Data Protection Impact Assessment
  - Sec. 35 — Automated Individual Decision-Making
  - Sec. 50 — Data Localisation Requirements
  - Sec. 63 — Penalties
- Note: a Data Protection (Amendment) Bill, 2025 is pending and would change Sec. 63's penalty calculation from "whichever is lower" to "whichever is higher," among other changes. This dataset reflects the Act as currently in force, not the proposed bill.

## South Korea AI Basic Act (Act No. 20676)

- Ministry of Science and ICT (MSIT), English portal: https://www.msit.go.kr/eng/
- Status: fully in force as of 22 January 2026, with a one-year grace period on enforcement. Core provisions confirmed (Art. 31 transparency, Art. 32 risk assessment) via multiple 2026 legal-practitioner summaries; not yet cross-checked directly against the official Korean statutory text (English translations are unofficial).

## China Generative AI Measures (and related administrative regulations)

- Official CAC notice: http://www.cac.gov.cn/2023-07/13/c_1690898327029107.htm
- Full English translation (China Law Translate, non-official but widely used by practitioners): https://www.chinalawtranslate.com/en/generative-ai-interim/
- Status: effective 15 August 2023, confirmed. Sits alongside (not instead of) the Algorithm Recommendation Provisions (2022) and Deep Synthesis Provisions (2023) — treat China's AI governance as a layered stack of administrative measures, not a single statute.

## Council of Europe Framework Convention on AI

- Official treaty page: https://www.coe.int/en/web/artificial-intelligence/the-framework-convention-on-artificial-intelligence
- Status: opened for signature September 2024; binds only ratifying states. Entries in this dataset are marked `"verified": false` pending direct article-by-article confirmation.

## Canada (post-AIDA landscape)

- Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada, AIDA companion document (historical — AIDA did not pass): https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/innovation-better-canada/en/artificial-intelligence-and-data-act-aida-companion-document
- Status: AIDA (Bill C-27) died on the order paper in January 2025 and has not been reintroduced as of mid-2026. Canada currently relies on PIPEDA, Quebec's Law 25, and a non-binding federal Directive on Automated Decision-Making.

## Brazil AI Bill (PL 2338/2023)

- Brazilian Federal Senate bill tracker: https://www25.senado.leg.br/web/atividade/materias/-/materia/157233
- Status: Senate-approved 10 December 2024; pending in the Chamber of Deputies as of mid-2026 with no fixed enactment timeline. Legal status and penalty figures (BRL 50m / 2% turnover) confirmed via Library of Congress Global Legal Monitor and Brazilian law-firm client alerts.
